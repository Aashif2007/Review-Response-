Gym hours are 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM, Monday through Saturday.

Sunday hours are 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

Membership plans: Monthly, Quarterly, and Annual — all include full gym access.

Personal training sessions can be booked at the front desk or via the app.

Members should wipe down equipment after use.

Locker rentals are available for a small daily fee.
